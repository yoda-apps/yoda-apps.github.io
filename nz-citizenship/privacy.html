Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what the NZ Citizenship Test iPhone app ("the app") does with your information. The short version: the app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or other contact details. It sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, described below.

Who we are

The app is developed and published by Hanna Paziuk, an individual developer in Lithuania ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the information described in this policy (the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation).

Postal address: J. Savickio g. 4-7, LT-01108 Vilnius, Lithuania. Email: yoda26app@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The app keeps the following on your device only:

  • your answers, statistics, study streak and game scores;
  • questions you saved or missed;
  • your settings, such as chosen topics and a planned test date.

Your individual answers, saved questions and settings are not sent to us or to anyone else. They are removed when you delete the app or use Reset progress in the app settings.

Checking for updates

The app reads a small status file from our website to find out whether the official test questions have been published. This request does not include your name, your progress or any other personal information. Like any website, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) can see the IP address the request comes from.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To find out which parts of the app are used and to fix errors, the app uses two services from Google: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

  • Usage statistics: which screens and features are opened, when a quiz, mock test or game is started and finished and with what score, when a free daily limit is reached, and whether the subscription screen was shown and a plan was bought.
  • Crash reports: the technical details of an error, such as the place in the code where it happened, the iPhone model and the iOS version.
  • Technical details: a random identifier created for this installation of the app, the app version, the language and the country or region worked out from the internet connection.

This information is not linked to your name, email address or Apple Account, and we cannot use it to find out who you are. We do not use the advertising identifier and do not use this information for advertising. The identifier is reset when you delete the app. Google processes the information on our behalf as described in the Firebase privacy information, and its servers may be outside New Zealand.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not show advertising or use advertising services.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are sold and billed by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription or purchase is active. Apple's handling of your information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can send you alerts, such as when the official test questions are published. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and do not add you to any mailing list.

Children

The app is a general study tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, we will update this page and change the date above before the change takes effect.
