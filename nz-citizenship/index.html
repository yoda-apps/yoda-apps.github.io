Get ready for the New Zealand citizenship test

Practice questions, plain-English guides and mock tests on every announced topic, in one iPhone app. 1,000+ practice questions, each linked to its official source.

Coming soon to the App Store

Practice questions

More than 1,000 questions across eight chapters: government, elections, rights, history, symbols, daily life, travel and regions.

Mock tests

Timed tests of 20 questions with a 75% pass mark, so you know when you are ready.

Guides

Short plain-English guides and summaries of official documents, each with its sources.

Play and learn

Quick games: true or false, guess the place from a photo, and a memory game.

The practice questions are based on the topics announced for the test. They are not official questions. The app will let you know when the official questions are published.
