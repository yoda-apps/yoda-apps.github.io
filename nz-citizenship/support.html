Support

We usually reply within two working days.

Email us at yoda26app@gmail.com. It helps if you tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

If you bought Premium and it is not active, open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Mistakes in questions or guides

If you spot a mistake, email us with the question text. Every question and guide links to its source.

Your progress

Your answers, statistics and saved questions are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also clear them in the app under Settings, Reset progress.
